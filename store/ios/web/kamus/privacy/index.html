Privacy policy

Discover Turkish, one word at a time.

Updated: October 27, 2026

Dictionary data on your device

Kâmus does not require an account. Search words, timestamps, search counts and favorites are stored in a local SQLite database. The iOS app and its own widget and share extensions may access this data through an App Group. The app does not send your history or favorites to a developer-operated server.

Online dictionary requests

When online, your search word is sent over HTTPS to the Turkish Language Association service at sozluk.gov.tr to retrieve updated definitions. The service provider can see your IP address and standard connection information. TDK is responsible for its own service practices. Kâmus is an independent app and is not an official TDK product.

Clipboard and selected text

When the iOS app becomes active, it may check for a short word on the clipboard and offer a search. iOS controls clipboard access and may ask permission. The suggestion is not sent as a dictionary query until you choose to search it. Choosing the Kâmus share action processes the selected text for a dictionary lookup. Android’s selected-text action similarly passes your chosen word to Kâmus.

Speech, widgets and tracking

Speech uses the operating system’s speech service. Widgets use local dictionary data and links into the app. The current mobile app code includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Apple and Google provide store, operating system and diagnostics services under their own terms.

Managing your data

On iOS, delete individual entries or clear the History tab; clearing all history also clears favorites in the current version. Removing a favorite star removes that favorite. Android history controls depend on the installed version. Uninstalling removes local app data; operating system backups and shared copies must be managed separately.
