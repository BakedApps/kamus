Kâmus support

Discover Turkish, one word at a time.

Updated: October 27, 2026

Does it work offline?

The bundled local dictionary is available offline. Online searches retrieve updated results from the TDK service. Check spelling and connectivity if a word is missing.

How do I hear a word?

Tap the speaker on a word card. Check your volume and installed Turkish speech voices. Speech uses the operating system’s speech service.

How do I manage favorites and history?

Tap the star to add or remove a favorite. On iOS, swipe a history row to delete it, or choose Temizle to clear all history. Clearing all history also removes favorites.

How is Word of the Day chosen?

A word is selected by date from the dictionary on your device. Open Kâmus once, then check the Word of the Day tab or widget.

How do I look up selected text?

On iOS, select text and choose Kâmus’ta Ara from Share. Edit the action list if needed. On Android, choose Kâmus from the text selection menu. Availability depends on the app you are reading in.

How do I add a widget?

Enter Home Screen editing on iPhone or iPad, choose Add Widget and select Kâmus. Lock Screen widgets are also available on supported devices.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@bakedapps.cloud with your app version, iOS/Android version, device model and steps to reproduce the problem. Hide personal information in screenshots.
